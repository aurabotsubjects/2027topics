# The Braided Map: ChatGPT Image Prompts

The app already looks finished without any images, because every mountain, river, star field, paddock, character and artefact is drawn in code. Images you make in ChatGPT are an optional upgrade that fades in over the built-in art.

## How to use these images
1. Paste the **STYLE LOCK** first, then add the prompt for the image you want.
2. Save the image as **WebP** (or PNG, then convert it) using the **exact file name** shown in this file.
3. Put the file in `hakatere/assets/img/`. The app finds it automatically and fades it in. If a file is missing, the app uses its built-in art instead, so nothing breaks.

> **Batch 1 — used in Week 1 now (13 images):** the six characters, `hero-w01`, the five subject banners and the Week 1 artefacts marked ⭐.
> **Batch 2 and 3 — later weeks:** the other hero scenes and artefacts. Make them whenever you like; they are already wired in by file name and will appear as each week is built.

**Where each image shows up**
| File | Where it appears |
|---|---|
| `char-*.webp` | The guide's portrait on every Field Note slide (and the Dealer's Offer on Fridays) |
| `hero-wNN.webp` | Behind Monday's Field Note, behind Friday's Survey Seal, on the home-page week tile and the Week page header |
| `banner-*.webp` | The day cards on the Week page and the header of each lesson page |
| `art-*.webp` | Artefact pictures on slides and in interactives (e.g. the Source Sorter's stone cottage) |

## STYLE LOCK (paste this first, every time)
> Sophisticated layered cut-paper diorama illustration, museum-exhibition quality, not childish and not "farm cartoon". Five to seven physical paper layers with soft, realistic cast shadows between them, giving real depth. Matte card and cream survey paper with subtle grain, fine fibres and gently torn edges. Canterbury high-country light: clear, crisp and golden, with gentle atmospheric haze between distant mountain layers (far ranges paler and bluer). Palette strictly limited to: field-book cream #F3ECDD, blue-black ink #1F2A30, greywacke grey #4B5357, river-shingle grey #A7A59C, glacier blue #BFE0EA, river blue #3D8DB3, tussock gold #C9A04A, wheat #E6C873, paddock green #5E7F3A, trig red #B4412F, nor'west cream #EADBB8, and deep night indigo #121A36 for night scenes. Recurring motifs used lightly: fine silver-blue braided-river threads that split and rejoin, faint survey grid lines and contour lines, small red trig triangles. Precise, elegant, editorial. No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no brand marks, no watermarks. No cartoon faces, no googly eyes, no thick black outlines, no glossy 3D plastic look.

**Never include (blueprint §15, §17):** real people (living or historical), Māori ancestors or tūpuna, carvings, kōwhaiwhai or tukutuku patterns, rock-art figures, marae buildings, the Scratch Cat, brand logos or real machinery company badges or colours that identify a brand.

### Rules for all images
- Fictional characters only. No real people, living or historical (not Joseph Thomas, Lord Ashburton, Walter Mantell, Rita Angus or anyone else).
- No Māori ancestors, carvings, kōwhaiwhai, tukutuku, rock-art figures or marae buildings. Māori visual culture appears in the app only as approved content, never decoration (blueprint §10.1, §17).
- No brand logos or badges on machinery, clothing or packaging.
- Check every image before use: if ChatGPT adds text, letters or a pattern that looks culturally specific, regenerate it.
- Credit line for the Unit Plan: "Illustrations generated with ChatGPT from prompts written for The Braided Map; procedural artwork drawn in code."
